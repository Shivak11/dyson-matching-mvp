Dyson's [India Big Ball specification](https://www.dyson.in/big-ball-vacuum) identifies the catalogue product as CY23 with 2 Tier Radial cyclones. Its [CY23 manual](https://www.dyson.co.uk/content/dam/dyson/india/2025/CY23_IN_DYSON-BIG-BALL.pdf) requires filter washing. The ordinary Big Ball and Cinetic filtration distinction does not justify merging all ball-shaped cylinder models, and cyclone tier count alone is not exclusive.

The [Gen5detect product page](https://www.dyson.com/vacuum-cleaners/cordless/gen5/detect-hepa-purple-iron) documents button power, an LCD and HEPA filtration. The [Gen5 manual](https://www.dyson.co.uk/content/dam/dyson/maintenance/user-guides/en_my/N418_OUG_MY.pdf) documents its silver Selection button. Its existing manufacturer image also shows a cyan rear filter. The [V16 page](https://www.dyson.in/v16-piston-animal-cordless-vacuum) documents its compression mechanism and shared broad features. The exact Gen5 upper red-button geometry has not been verified from a rendered manufacturer rear view; PDF screenshots failed. No exclusive geometry rule may be inferred from that gap.

Manufacturer compatibility is positive evidence for a set. Hair screw tool [972213-02](https://www.dyson.com/support/journey/spare-details.972213-02) lists Gen5detect, V12 Detect Slim and Gen5outsize; [971426-01](https://www.dyson.com/support/journey/spare-details.971426-01) lists several other families. A missing entry is not proof of incompatibility. A readable part code may identify a component while leaving the cleaner body unresolved.
